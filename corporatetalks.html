Data and indicators from our ECB research paper:
Michele Ca' Zorzi, Gianluigi Lopardo, Ana-Simona Manu, Verba volant, transcripta manent: what corporate earnings calls reveal about the AI stock rally

Overview

The Corporate Talks dashboard measures thematic discussion in S&P 500 earnings call transcripts from 2014 Q1 to present (updated quarterly). It reports the share of each transcript devoted to selected topics (Exposure). The “All Topics” view displays the top 10 topics by average exposure.

Exposure: Percentage of transcript paragraphs devoted to a given topic.

Method: Topic detection via zero‑shot classification (BART-large-MNLI) applied to 22,590+ transcripts. Exposure = topic paragraphs / total transcript paragraphs (length-normalised).
