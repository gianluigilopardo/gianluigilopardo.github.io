GenAI Exposure Analysis

Interactive visualization of US corporate GenAI exposure and sentiment trends

Data and indicators from our research paper:
Michele Ca’ Zorzi, Gianluigi Lopardo, Ana-Simona Manu, Verba volant, transcripta manent: what corporate earnings calls reveal about the AI stock rally

Last data update: January 2025
Exposure = share of a quarterly earnings call transcript devoted to GenAI (GenAI-related content / total transcript content).
Sentiment (Risk, Adoption, Opportunity) reflects the directional tone of GenAI-related discussion segments.
