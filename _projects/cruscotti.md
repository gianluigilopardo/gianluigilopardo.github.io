The **Italian Municipalities Dashboard** lets users compare up to twelve Italian municipalities across demographic, economic, and administrative indicators.

- **84 summary indicators**, with 47 additional extended indicators available on demand.
- **Flexible comparisons**: choose municipalities and indicators, then sort by value.
- **Public data sources**: datasets from several public bodies, brought together by [Cruscotto Italia · AgID](https://cruscotto-italia.dati.gov.it/).

The dashboard is currently available in Italian only. Its content is distributed under the [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license.
